Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

PromoPayoff ("the app") is developed by Roy Altman ("I," "me"). This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app. The short version: I don't collect any of your data.

Information you enter

The card details you enter (card names, balances, credit limits, APRs, promo end dates and notes) are stored:

  • on your device, and
  • if you're signed into iCloud, in your own private iCloud database, so your cards sync between your iPhone and Mac.

Your private iCloud data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. I can't see, access or recover it.

The app does not ask for or store bank logins, account numbers, or card numbers, and it does not connect to your bank.

Information I don't collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No data is sent to servers I operate. The app has no server of its own.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

The app can lock itself with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Authentication is handled entirely by Apple's operating system. The app never receives or stores your biometric data.

Purchases

The optional Full Unlock purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. I don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks with the App Store whether the unlock has been purchased.

Settings

Preferences such as your pay frequency (weekly or every two weeks) are stored only on your device.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a card in the app to remove it from your device and from your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.
  • To remove the app's iCloud data entirely, use your device's iCloud storage settings (Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
