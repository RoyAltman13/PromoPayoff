Common questions

How is "Needed per Pay" calculated?

PromoPayoff counts the paychecks left before your promo end date, based on your pay frequency (weekly or every two weeks), and divides your current balance by that number. A partial pay period counts as one, so you finish on or before the deadline. "Needed per Month" works the same way with months.

How do I switch between weekly and biweekly pay?

Tap the gear icon on the main screen to open Settings, then choose Weekly or Every 2 Weeks under "I get paid." All per-pay amounts update right away. The setting is saved separately on each device.

How does Allocate split my payment?

First, each promo card gets the amount it needs to stay on track (scaled down proportionally if your payment doesn't cover them all). Anything left goes to your regular-APR cards, either highest APR first or split by APR, your choice. If money is still left after that, it goes to the promo that ends soonest.

My cards aren't syncing between my iPhone and Mac.

Make sure both devices are signed into the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for PromoPayoff (Settings > your name > iCloud). Sync needs an internet connection and can take a few minutes. Without iCloud, each device keeps its own separate list of cards.

I bought the Full Unlock but Payments and Allocate are still locked.

Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchase." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple Account you used to buy it. The unlock works on both iPhone and Mac with that account.

Is the Full Unlock a subscription?

No. It's a one-time $0.99 purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does PromoPayoff connect to my bank or card accounts?

No. You enter your balances yourself. The app never asks for bank logins or card numbers. Use Payments to update a balance after you pay.

How do I delete my data?

On iPhone, swipe left on a card to delete it. Deleting a card removes it from your devices and your iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for more.

Face ID isn't working.

Tap Unlock to try again, or use your device passcode. Check that Face ID or Touch ID is set up in your device's Settings.
